Every Tongue

In Mandarin, the pitch of a word isn't just tone of voice — it's part of the word's meaning. When a hymn's melody doesn't match those pitches, the lyrics can quietly turn into different words. Every Tongue finds exactly where that happens, and suggests a fix.

One example
"Amazing Grace," sung in Mandarin
奇异恩典，何等甘甜
何 "how" → sung on this melody → 贺 "congratulate"
