When the tune fights the tone

In most of the world's languages, pitch is part of the word. When churches sing translated Western hymns, the melody overrides the words, and the lyrics stop meaning what the translator intended. This tool takes an existing song and a target language and finds the translation that is both faithful and singable against that exact melody — then shows you the difference.
