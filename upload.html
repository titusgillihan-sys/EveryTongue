Analyze your own line

Paste a short Mandarin lyric line, pick (or hand-build) a melody shape, and the same contrary-motion scorer used in the library flags every place the tune fights the words — then proposes ranked replacements that stay inside a meaning budget you control. Tones come from a ~150-word starter dictionary; unknown characters are marked, never guessed.
